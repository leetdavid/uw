---
title: Automatic organization
description: Let uw file your tabs into tasks as you browse, and name the tasks it starts. It uses your own AI provider.
---

## Turn it *on*

1. Pick a decision model, a language model, or both. See [Add an AI provider](./add-an-ai-provider.mdx).
2. Open `uw://settings/uwOrganization` and turn on the switch at the top.
3. Browse as usual.

For each tab, uw sends your models its title, address, the tab it came from, your task names, and a short excerpt of its text. A terminal tab sends its title and folder, never what the shell printed. Pinned tabs are never sorted.

## What *moves*

When the model is sure enough, uw moves the tab, with the tabs under it, and a message such as "Moved to Plan Lisbon trip" shows above **Organize** for a few seconds, with **Undo**. Nothing shows when the tab stays put.

- A tab that fits one of your tasks moves into it.
- A one-off page that doesn't fit its task, such as a sign-in page, moves to the Scratchpad.
- A page that starts something of its own moves at once into a new task, "New task". Tabs under it go along, unless they still fit the old task.

To keep a tab where it is, right-click it and choose **Keep Here**. An anchor marks its row, and uw leaves it alone. Click the anchor to let it go.

Three sliders on the settings page set how sure the model must be:

| Slider | What it decides | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Move a tab** | Moving a tab into or out of a task | 0.70 |
| **Start a task** | Starting a new task | 0.55 |
| **Rename a task** | Giving a task uw named a wider name | 0.70 |

Each runs from 0.50 to 0.95. Lower one and uw does that more often, and is wrong more often.

## How tasks *get names*

With a language model set, "New task" takes a name within a few seconds of a page that starts a piece of work. A sign-in page, a home page, or a feed names nothing.

A task uw named can take a wider name as related pages join it. A name you typed is never replaced; a lock on the row shows it. Click the lock to hand the name back to uw.

**Name Task**, in the task's menu or the **Tasks** menu, asks for a name now, even while automatic organization is paused or off. If you type a name meanwhile, yours wins.

## Pause it and *watch it work*

Click **Organize** at the foot of the sidebar. Its first line says what automatic organization is doing.

- **Pause** offers **1 hour**, **Until restart**, and **Until I resume**. While paused, the row reads **Resume**.
- **Undo Move** reverses the last move. See [Undo a move](./undo-a-move.mdx).
- **Show Activity** shows what the model answered for each tab and how sure it was. A move that fell short of its slider gets **Confirm**, which carries it out.
- **Settings…** opens the settings page.
