---
title: Collapse a branch
description: Hide the tabs under a page while you don't need them. They stay open and come back where they were.
---

## Collapse it and *expand it again*

1. Point at a tab that has tabs under it. Its favicon turns into a chevron.
2. Click the chevron. The tabs under it hide, and its row ends with the number of tabs it hides.
3. Click the chevron again to show them.

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>.</kbd> does the same for the selected tab. You can also right-click a tab, or open the **Tab** menu, and choose **Collapse Branch**. Once the branch is collapsed, the item reads **Expand Branch**.

## While it's *collapsed*

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd> skips the hidden tabs.
- Collapsing the branch of the tab you're on selects the page at its top.
- <kbd>Cmd</kbd>-click another link on that page, and the new tab stays hidden while the number goes up. A link you open in front expands the branch.
- Going to a hidden tab another way, such as with <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd>, expands the branch.
- Drag the collapsed tab, and its hidden tabs move with it. It stays collapsed.
- Close the collapsed tab, and its tabs show again, one level up.

The branch stays collapsed when you quit and open uw again. Moves by [automatic organization](./automatic-organization.mdx), and undoing them, leave it collapsed.
