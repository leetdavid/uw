---
title: Command bar
description: Open pages, switch tabs, ask uw, or run a shell command from one box, without leaving the keyboard.
---

## Open a page *from the keyboard*

1. Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>. A panel opens over the page. **File → New Tab** and the **+** at the foot of the sidebar open it too.
2. Type a few letters of a page you visited or have open.
3. Press <kbd>Return</kbd>, or pick another row with the arrow keys or the mouse.

The rows are your open tabs, history, bookmarks, and a search for what you typed, labeled **Tab**, **History**, **Bookmark**, and **Search**. A **Tab** row switches to that tab. Any other row opens in a new tab, where <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>'s blank tab would have gone. Text with no row picked opens as an address, or as a search.

<kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the panel and opens nothing.

<Callout>
  The panel shows nothing until you type, and nothing you type goes to your search engine until you pick a row.
</Callout>

## Complete *an address*

Type part of an address you've typed twice before, such as `githu`. The rest appears after the caret, selected. <kbd>Tab</kbd> makes it typed text, and <kbd>Return</kbd> opens it.

Type a site search keyword and press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to search that site, as in Chromium's address bar.

## Ask uw or *run a command*

- <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd> asks uw. A [chat](./chats.mdx) opens with your text as its first message.
- To ask about the page you're on, type `@` first. A chip shows the page's icon and title, and <kbd>Return</kbd> asks uw with that page as the agent's tab 1. The agent reads it first and can act in it, with the same approvals as in its own tabs. <kbd>Backspace</kbd> in the empty field removes the chip. On a chat, a terminal, or a uw page, `@` is just text.
- <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd> runs the text as a shell command in a new [terminal tab](./terminal-tabs.mdx).
- Type `>` first, or open the panel with <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>P</kbd>, and <kbd>Return</kbd> runs the text as a shell command the same way.

## Turn it *off*

Turn off **Cmd+T opens the command bar** under **Command bar** at `uw://settings/uwOrganization`. <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> then opens a blank tab again, in every window.
