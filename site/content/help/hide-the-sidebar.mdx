---
title: Hide the sidebar
description: Give the page the whole window. The sidebar waits at the left edge, and slides in over the page when you need a tab.
---

## Hide it and *show it again*

1. Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>, or choose **View → Hide Sidebar**. The sidebar and the window's buttons go, and the page fills the window inside a thin edge of your theme.
2. To bring it back for good, press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> again, or choose **View → Show Sidebar**.

## Reach a tab *without showing it*

Rest the pointer on the window's left edge. After a moment the sidebar slides in over the page as a card, with the window's buttons at its top left. The page doesn't move.

Pick a tab and the card stays. Move the pointer onto the page, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, and it slides away.

<Callout>
  While the sidebar is hidden, uw's move messages show on their own over the page's bottom-left corner, with **Undo**.
</Callout>
