## Open *a little window*

Press <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>, in uw or in any other app while uw is running. A little window opens on the desktop you're looking at, with the [command bar](./command-bar.mdx). Type a search or an address and press <kbd>Return</kbd>, and the page opens in the little window. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> before you pick anything, and the window closes again.

**New Little Window** is also in the **File** menu and in the menu of the **▾** at the bottom of the sidebar. Each one opens another little window.

To open a link in a little window, hold <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> as you click it, or right-click it and choose **Open Link in Little Window**.

A little window has no sidebar, and it isn't in any workspace. It takes the theme of the workspace of the window you used last. Click the address, or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd>, to go somewhere else in it.

## Keep *the page*

Click **Move to** and your workspace's name at the right of the toolbar, or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd>. The page moves to the top of the Scratchpad in your workspace's window, as it is: what you scrolled to, what you typed, and its back and forward pages come along. The little window closes, and [automatic organization](./automatic-organization.mdx) files the tab from there.

To move the page to another workspace, click the **▾**, or press <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd>, and pick one. A workspace on another profile opens the page's address there instead.

A link in a little window that opens a new tab, such as a <kbd>Cmd</kbd>-click, opens at the top of the Scratchpad in your workspace's window. The little window stays in front.

## Close *it*

Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd>. <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> brings it back as a little window. Little windows don't come back after you quit uw.

A little window you haven't used for 6 hours closes on its own, unless its page is playing sound or would ask before you leave it.

## Settings

Under **Little windows** at `uw://settings/uwOrganization`:

- **Open a little window with Option+Cmd+N in uw** and **from other apps** turn the key on or off in each place. While uw takes the key from other apps, they don't get it: Finder's New Smart Folder and Mail's New Viewer Window, for example. Change the key under **Keyboard shortcuts** at `uw://settings/shortcuts`.
- **Open links from other apps in a little window** opens links from Mail, Slack, or a terminal in a little window instead of at the top of the Scratchpad. It's off at first.
- **Close a little window after it's unused for** picks 1 hour, 6 hours, 12 hours, 1 day, or Never.

## On Windows

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>, and use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> where the Mac uses <kbd>Cmd</kbd>: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd> keeps the page and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd> lists the workspaces. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Alt</kbd>-click opens a link in a little window.

On a keyboard that types a letter with <kbd>AltGr</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>, such as "ń" on a Polish one, uw leaves <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd> to other apps, since Windows sends AltGr as Ctrl+Alt. Settings says so under the switch. The key still works in uw.
