---
title: Mini player
description: Keep a video playing while you use other tabs. It docks at the bottom of the sidebar, and floats over other apps when the sidebar is out of sight.
---

## Start *the mini player*

Use the site's picture-in-picture button, or right-click the video twice and choose **Picture in Picture**.

Or switch tabs. A playing video with sound comes with you into the mini player, and goes back into its page when you return to its tab. A muted or small video stays in its page.

While a uw window is in front with its sidebar showing, the video docks at the bottom of the sidebar and keeps playing as you switch tabs. Click another uw window and it moves there. When the sidebar is hidden, or another app's windows cover it, the video floats in the corner of the screen, over every app. Come back to uw and it docks again.

## Control *the video*

Point at the video:

- Close and back to tab are in the top corners.
- Back 10 seconds, play or pause, and forward 10 seconds are in the middle.
- The time, mute, and a progress bar you can drag are at the bottom.
- **Pop out** floats a docked video until you dock it again. On a floating video, **Dock in sidebar** brings it back.

Press and hold the video to play it at 2× while you hold. Drag a floating video to move it.

## More than *one at a time*

Put a second video in the mini player, and the first pauses and peeks out above it. Point at the docked player to see the paused videos as rows. Click one to play it, or its × to drop it. Up to three stack up.

A site's own small window, such as a Google Meet call, docks too, under any video, and keeps running while videos come and go. Point at it and click **Live** to turn that off for its site. It then pauses and waits like a video when another video comes.

## Playback controls *and settings*

When a tab you're not looking at plays sound outside the mini player, a row above the sidebar's foot shows it, with pause, and previous and next when the site offers them. Click its title to go to the tab. The × on its corner pauses it and hides the row.

Under **Mini player** at `uw://settings/uwOrganization`:

- **Picture in picture**: **Dock in the sidebar**, the default. **Don't dock** keeps a video in its page when you switch tabs, and floats one you put in picture in picture yourself. **Off** turns picture in picture off, so a site's picture-in-picture button does nothing.
- **Open the mini player when you switch tabs**.
- **Float the mini player when the sidebar is covered**. Turn it off to keep the video docked while a uw window shows.
- **Live windows** lists every site that has opened its own small window, each with a **Live** switch.
