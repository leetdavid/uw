---
title: More than one window
description: Every window shows the same tabs and tasks. A page runs in one window at a time, and closing a window closes none of its tabs.
---

## Open *another window*

Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>. The new window shows the same tasks and tabs as your other windows, in the same order, and opens the [command bar](./command-bar.mdx) over a blank tab. Pick another tab or close the window, and the blank tab goes too, so blank tabs don't pile up.

Each window has its own selected tab. Everything else you do in one window happens in all of them: opening, closing, moving, and nesting tabs, and naming, coloring, and collapsing tasks.

When you collapse a task in one window, it collapses in the others too, but a window whose selected tab is inside keeps that tab's row in view until you pick another tab there.

## Use a tab *in two windows*

A page runs in one window at a time. Select its tab in another window, and the page moves there with everything on it, such as text you haven't sent yet.

The window it left shows a dimmed picture of the page with **Open here**. Switch back to that window, or click **Open here**, and the page comes back.

## Close *a window*

Closing a window closes none of its tabs. They stay in your other windows.

When you close the last window, uw keeps your tabs. Pages that are doing nothing unload. Pages still at work keep running: one playing sound, one with text you haven't sent, a terminal running a program, and a tab the agent works in. Click uw in the Dock or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>, and every tab is back.

Closing the last tab, other than pinned tabs, closes every window. <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> brings back tabs you closed, but not a window, since closing one loses nothing.

## Drag *a tab out*

Drag a tab out of the sidebar onto the desktop, and a new window opens showing it. The tab keeps its place in the list. Drag it back into the first window, and the new window closes, unless you used it for another tab.

Drag a tab into a window on another workspace to move it there.

<Callout>
  Private windows keep their own tabs and share none.
</Callout>
