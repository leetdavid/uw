---
title: New tab page
description: Search or ask from a blank tab, and see what uw is doing for you.
---

A blank tab shows uw's new tab page: a box to search or ask. It opens from **New blank tab** in the [command bar](./command-bar.mdx), from <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> when the command bar is off, and from **New Child Tab**, **New Tab Below**, and a task's **New Tab**.

## Search *or go to a page*

1. Type a few letters of a page you visited or have open, or an address.
2. Pick a row with the arrow keys or the mouse, or press <kbd>Return</kbd>.

The page opens in this tab. Text with no row picked opens as an address, or searches with your search engine.

<Callout>
  The rows come from your open tabs, history, and bookmarks. Nothing you type goes to your search engine until you press <kbd>Return</kbd>.
</Callout>

## Ask *uw*

1. Click **Ask**, or press <kbd>Tab</kbd> in the box.
2. Type what you want done, such as "Find a tram day pass for Lisbon".
3. Press <kbd>Return</kbd>.

This tab becomes a [chat](./chats.mdx) with your text as its first message. The pages the agent opens go under it. The model chip picks the model for this chat. **Once** makes it a [scheduled chat](./scheduled-chats.mdx) that runs again on its own.

**Ask** is grayed until you [add an AI provider](./add-an-ai-provider.mdx).

## In a *private window*

The page shows only the box. **Ask** starts a private chat, which uw forgets when the private windows close, and it has no **Once**.
