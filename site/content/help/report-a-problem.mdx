title: Report a problem
description: Tell the uw team what went wrong, and choose whether uw sends crash and problem reports.
---

## Open *an issue*

1. Choose **Help → Report an Issue…**. You can also click **Report an issue** in **Settings → Privacy and security**.
2. A new GitHub issue opens with your uw, Chromium, and macOS versions filled in. Sign in to GitHub if it asks.
3. Write the exact steps, what you expected, and what happened instead. Say whether it happened in a new profile or one you've used for a while.
4. For a problem with layout, dragging, focus, or tabs coming back after a restart, attach a screenshot or a short screen recording.

## After *a crash*

Say in the issue when uw quit and what you were doing. If you chose to send reports, uw has already sent the crash report, and the time helps match it to your issue.

## Crash and problem reports *with your consent*

The first time you open uw, a card at the foot of the sidebar asks **Help fix uw?** With **Send reports**, uw sends:

- A crash report when uw crashes.
- Rage clicks, dead clicks, and JavaScript errors on uw's own pages, such as settings and chats. What you type there is masked.

Nothing from the websites you visit is ever watched. With **Don't send**, nothing is sent.

To change your answer, open **Settings → Privacy and security** and use the **Send crash and problem reports** switch under **Problem reports**.

<Callout type="warning">
  A crash report can hold parts of the pages that were open when uw crashed.
</Callout>
