---
title: Tabs under tabs
description: A link you open from a page lands just below it, one level in, so you can see where each tab came from. Move, nest, and close a page together with the tabs it opened.
---

## Links open *under their page*

<kbd>Cmd</kbd>-click a link, and the new tab opens directly below its page, indented one level. A page with tabs under it is a parent tab. Together they make a tab branch.

Close the parent with its close button, and the tabs under it stay open and move up a level. To close them too, right-click the parent and choose **Close Branch**, below **Close**. You can also Option-click its close button. Hold <kbd>Option</kbd> with the pointer on the close button, and the tabs that would close turn red first.

To open a blank tab under a tab, right-click it and choose **New Child Tab**, below **New Tab Below**. The new tab is selected and sits first under that tab, above its other tabs. **New Tab Below** opens a tab at the same level, after the tab's branch.

## Move *a whole branch*

Right-click a tab that opened other tabs:

- **Move to New Task** and **Move to Scratchpad** take the tabs it opened along.
- **Nest Under Previous Tab** puts it under the tab above. **Move Up One Level** takes it out one level.
- **Undo Tab Organization** reverses the last of these moves. See [Undo a move](./undo-a-move.mdx).

Drag a parent tab, and the tabs under it come along. Hold <kbd>Option</kbd> while you drag to move the tab alone; its tabs move up a level. With <kbd>Option</kbd> held over the parent, the tabs that would stay behind fade. While you drag, move the pointer right to nest the tab under the tab above, or left to take it out of a branch. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> before you let go to put it back.

**Drag sideways to nest tabs**, under **Mouse** at `uw://settings/uwOrganization`, turns the sideways moves off.

A tab you nest by hand, or open from a [chat](./chats.mdx), stays with its parent when uw organizes your tabs.

## Keys for *tabs and tasks*

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Nest the tab under the tab above |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> | Move it up one level |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move it and its tabs past the tab above or below |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> | New child tab |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>.</kbd> | [Collapse or expand](./collapse-a-branch.mdx) the branch |
| <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> | Close the branch |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd> | New task |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd> | Move to a new task |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> | Move to the Scratchpad |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> | [Keep the tab](./automatic-organization.mdx) where it is |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd> | Go to the tab you last used in that task |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>0</kbd> | Go to the Scratchpad |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> | Rename the task |
| <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo the last move |

Each command is also in the **Tab** and **Tasks** menus, with its key. The keys work in a terminal tab too. A page that uses one of these keys for itself keeps it. To change a key, choose **Help → Keyboard Shortcuts…**.
