---
title: Tasks
description: Keep the tabs of one piece of work together. Each task is a folder in the sidebar, and tabs outside every task sit in the Scratchpad.
---

## Start *a task*

1. Right-click a tab and choose **Move to New Task**. The tab, and the tabs it opened, move into a new task named "New task", after your other tasks.
2. Double-click "New task" on the task's row and type a name, or let uw [name it](./automatic-organization.mdx).

To start a task with an empty tab, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>, or click **▾** at the foot of the sidebar and choose **New Task**. The name field on its row is ready for you to type.

To move a tab into a task you already have, right-click it and choose **Move to Task**. <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> opens a tab beside the current one, in its task.

Tabs outside every task sit under **Scratchpad** at the foot of the tab list. Tasks don't go inside other tasks. Tabs nest under the page they came from instead: see [Tabs under tabs](./tabs-under-tabs.mdx), which also lists the keys for tasks.

With [automatic organization](./automatic-organization.mdx) on, uw files tabs into tasks for you.

## Rename *a task*

Double-click the task's name, type a new one, and press <kbd>Return</kbd> or click elsewhere. <kbd>Esc</kbd> puts the old name back. The task stays open or collapsed as it was.

**Name Task** asks your language model for a name instead. See [Automatic organization](./automatic-organization.mdx).

## Use the task's *menu*

Click **⋮** on the task's row, or right-click the row. The menu has:

- **Rename Task**, and **Name Task** when a language model is set.
- A row of color dots. The task's color is ringed; click another to change it.
- **New Tab in Task** and **Move Task to New Window**.
- **Move Tabs to Scratchpad**, which ends the task and keeps its tabs open.
- **Close Task**, which closes the task and its tabs.

The arrow keys move between rows, <kbd>Return</kbd> runs one, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the menu.

Point at a task's row and its folder icon turns into a chevron. Click it to collapse or open the task. Drag the row to move the whole task.

A closed task isn't saved, but <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> brings back one you just closed. When you quit and open uw again, your tasks come back with their tabs.
