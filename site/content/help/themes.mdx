title: Themes
description: Pick the colors of uw's window, and choose whether uw and the pages you visit are light or dark.

## Open *the theme picker*

Choose **View → Theme…**, or right-click empty space in the sidebar and choose **Theme…**. You can also click the **Theme** row at `uw://settings/uwOrganization`.

A panel opens beside the sidebar. The window changes as you pick, so you see each change right away.

## Pick *your colors*

- Click a suggested theme.
- Or drag a dot in the color field. **+** adds a dot, up to three, for a gradient. Drag a dot out of the field to remove it.
- **Intensity** grays the colors. **Grain** adds a fine texture around the page.

Text on the window stays readable: white on a dark color, near-black on a light one.

On macOS 26 or later, the picker also has **Transparency**, which lets the desktop show through the window, and **Glass**, which turns pinned tabs, the selected tab, and the buttons at the foot of the sidebar into glass.

Each workspace keeps its own theme. A color you pick in Chromium's **Customize Chromium** panel becomes a one-color theme.

## Light, dark, *and pages*

**Light**, **Dark**, and **Auto** set the theme's mode. **Auto** follows macOS.

In dark mode the window is black. Turn on **Theme colors in dark mode** to see your theme's colors, darkened, instead.

**Pages** sets everything outside the sidebar: websites, extensions, uw's and Chromium's own pages such as Settings, and Chromium's menus and bubbles. Leave it on **Theme** to match the theme, or pick **Light** or **Dark**.

For example, with **Light** above and **Pages** on **Dark**, the sidebar, its menus, and the picker stay light, while Settings, menus, and websites that have a dark look of their own turn dark.

<Callout>
  uw tells websites which mode you want. A site with no dark look of its own stays light.
</Callout>
