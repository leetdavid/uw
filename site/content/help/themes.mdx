## Open *the theme picker*

Choose **View → Theme…**, or right-click empty space in the sidebar and choose **Theme…**. You can also click the **Theme** row at `uw://settings/uwOrganization`.

A panel opens beside the sidebar. The window changes as you pick, so you see each change right away.

## Pick *your colors*

- Click a suggested theme.
- Or drag a dot in the color field. **+** adds a dot, up to three, for a gradient. Drag a dot out of the field to remove it.
- **Intensity** grays the colors. **Brightness** darkens them all evenly, down to black. **Grain** adds a fine texture around the page.
- For a black window, pick **Black** among the suggestions.

Text on the window stays readable: white on a dark color, near-black on a light one.

On macOS 26 or later, the picker also has **Transparency**, which lets the desktop show through the window, and **Glass**, which turns pinned tabs, the selected tab, and the buttons at the foot of the sidebar into glass. On Windows it has **Glass** only.

Each workspace keeps its own theme. A color you pick in Chromium's **Customize Chromium** panel becomes a one-color theme.

## Remove the *space around the page*

At `uw://settings/uwOrganization`, under the **Theme** row, turn off **Space around the page**. The page then runs to the window's top, right, and bottom edges, with no strip of your theme around it. It keeps its round corners beside the sidebar. On Windows, minimize, maximize, and close then sit at the window's top right, drawn as Windows draws them.

The switch is for all of uw, not for one theme, and every open window follows at once. With the sidebar [hidden](./hide-the-sidebar.mdx), the page fills the whole window.

## Light *and dark*

Your theme colors only the sidebar and the strip around the page. Pick **Black**, or lower **Brightness**, for a dark sidebar.

**Mode**, the first row of the picker, sets everything else: websites, extensions, uw's and Chromium's own pages such as the chat page and Settings, the command bar, the address bar's suggestions, and every menu, card, and panel, the picker and Tab Search included. **Auto** follows your Mac's appearance (on Windows, its light or dark mode). It is one setting for all of a profile's workspaces, and private windows follow it too. Chromium's Settings → Appearance → **Mode** and the **Customize Chromium** panel's mode buttons change the same setting.

For example, with a lilac theme and **Mode** on **Dark**, the sidebar stays lilac while pages, menus, and panels turn dark.
