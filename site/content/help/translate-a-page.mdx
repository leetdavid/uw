---
title: Translate a page
description: Translate a page on your Mac, with Google Cloud Translation, or with your AI provider. Pick the engine once in Settings, then right-click a page.
---

uw translates pages with the engine you pick. Until you pick one, nothing translates, and uw never offers to translate by itself.

## Pick *an engine*

1. Right-click a page and choose **Set Up Translation…**, or open **Settings › Languages**.
2. Under **Translate pages with**, pick an engine.

| Engine | Where the page's text goes | Languages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This device | Nowhere. It stays on your Mac. | About 40, from Arabic to Vietnamese |
| Google Cloud Translation | To Google, with your own API key | Over 130 |
| AI provider | To your [AI provider](./add-an-ai-provider.mdx) | Whatever your model knows |

**This device** translates between any two of its languages.

**Google Cloud Translation** needs a Google Cloud API key with the Cloud Translation API turned on. Paste it under **API key** and click **Save**. uw translates one word to check it, and if Google turns the key down, Google's reason shows under the field. The key is kept on this device and used by every profile. Google bills your Cloud account for what it translates.

**AI provider** translates with a language model from a provider you added. Pick it under **Model**. The menu starts on the model that names your tasks.

## Translate *a page*

Right-click the page and choose **Translate to English**. The page's text changes in place, links and buttons keep working, and text that appears later, such as new posts in a feed, is translated as it shows up.

On a page in another language, the translate icon shows at the right of the address. Click it to translate the page or to pick another language. **Translate…** in the ⋮ menu does the same.

The first page in a language downloads that language to your Mac, about 80 MB. The translate bubble shows how far the download has got, and the page translates once it's done.

To see the page as it was, click the translate icon and choose **Show original**.

## Use *another engine once*

Right-click the page and choose **Translate With**, then an engine. The page translates with it this one time, and the next page uses your engine again. Engines you haven't set up are grey, and **Set Up Translation…** at the bottom opens the settings.

## Translate *a selection*

Select some text, right-click it, and choose **Translate “…”**. A bubble shows the translation and leaves the page as it is. **Translate With** below it translates the selection with another engine, this one time.

Google Cloud Translation and AI providers tell what language the selection is in. This device takes it to be in the page's language. If it isn't, choose **Selection Not in…** in the bubble's ⋮ menu and pick its language.

## Translate *a language every time*

Click the translate icon, then the bubble's ⋮ menu, and choose **Always translate Japanese**, or whichever language the page is in. Pages in that language then translate by themselves in normal windows. **Settings › Languages** lists these languages under **Automatically translate these languages**, and the languages uw never translates under **Never offer to translate these languages**.

## When this device *lacks a language*

If the page is in a language this device doesn't know, the bubble says so, for example "This device can't translate Swahili". With Google Cloud Translation or an AI provider set up, it offers to translate the page with it this one time. Otherwise it offers **Set up another engine…**.

If Google or your provider turns a page down, the bubble says so, with their reason under the title.

## Remove *a downloaded language*

**Settings › Languages** lists the languages this device has downloaded, with their size, under **Downloaded languages**. Click the bin beside one to delete it. It downloads again the next time a page needs it.

## Private *windows*

Private windows translate with the same engine, so Google Cloud Translation and your AI provider receive the text of pages and selections you translate there too. Languages you always translate don't translate by themselves in private windows.
