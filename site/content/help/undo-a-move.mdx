---
title: Undo a move
description: Put tabs back where they were after uw moves them, or after you move a branch by mistake.
---

## Undo it *right away*

When uw moves a tab, a message above **Organize** at the foot of the sidebar names the move for a few seconds. Click **Undo** on it.

While the sidebar is hidden, the message shows over the page's bottom-left corner instead, with the same **Undo**. See [Hide the sidebar](./hide-the-sidebar.mdx).

## Undo it *later*

Any of these reverses the last move:

- Press <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd>.
- Click **Organize**, then **Undo Move**. It names the move it reverses, such as "SFO to LIS flights → Plan Lisbon trip". With nothing to undo, it's grayed and says "Nothing to undo".
- Right-click a tab and choose **Undo Tab Organization**, or choose it from the **Tasks** menu.

## What undo *covers*

Undo reverses uw's moves and the moves you make with the branch commands, such as **Move to New Task**, **Move to Scratchpad**, **Nest Under Previous Tab**, and **Move Up One Level**. See [Tabs under tabs](./tabs-under-tabs.mdx).

- It works from the moment uw starts a new task, even while uw is still naming it. Undo then drops the request for a name.
- It doesn't reopen pages you closed.
- Naming a task, collapsing a branch, and keeping a tab aren't moves, so undo leaves them as they are.
