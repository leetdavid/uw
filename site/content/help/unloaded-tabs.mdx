## When a tab *unloads*

A tab that has been out of sight for 6 hours unloads, unless you've gone back to it more than 5 times. Time your Mac is asleep doesn't count. The tab keeps its place, title, and icon, and a dotted circle appears around its icon.

Click an unloaded tab, and its page loads again, as it would after you quit and open uw.

## What *stays loaded*

- Pinned tabs, and the tab each window shows.
- Terminal tabs and chats.
- Tabs the agent is working in, until it stops.
- Pages playing sound or in the mini player, and pages using your camera, microphone, or screen, or a Bluetooth or USB device.
- Pages that can send you notifications, and pages whose title or icon keeps changing, such as a timer.
- Pages with text you haven't sent, and pages with DevTools open.

## Change *when tabs unload*

Open `uw://settings/performance` and find **Memory Saver**.

- Pick **Balanced** to unload tabs after 4 hours, or **Maximum** for 2 hours. **Moderate** is the default.
- Turn **Memory Saver** off to keep tabs loaded.
- Under **Always keep these sites active**, add sites whose tabs should never unload.
- Turn off **Inactive tabs appearance** to hide the dotted circle.
