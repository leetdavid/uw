## What the agent *can do*

Ask in a chat, such as "Close the flight tabs I don't need any more" or "Put my hotel tabs in a task of their own". The agent can:

- list your tabs in every workspace, by task, with when you last looked at each,
- show you one of them,
- move tabs to a task, to a new task, or to the Scratchpad,
- rename a task,
- close tabs, or a task with all its tabs.

It sees each tab's title and address, never the page. To read one of your pages, it opens the address in a tab of its own.

Tabs move only within their workspace. The agent never closes a pinned tab or a terminal that is running a command, and never moves a pinned tab.

## When it *asks*

| Mode | Asks before |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask first** | Every move, rename, and close. |
| **Auto** | Closing your tabs. |
| **Full auto** | Nothing. |

The card names the tabs, such as `Close 5 tabs in Flights?`. Tabs the agent opened itself close without asking. See [Approvals](./approvals.mdx).

## Taking it *back*

A move counts as yours: the organizer leaves those tabs where the agent put them. <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> undoes it, as for any move. See [Undo a move](./undo-a-move.mdx).

A task the agent names keeps that name, with the lock, as if you typed it. Undo leaves a name alone.

**History → Recently Closed** brings back a tab the agent closed.

## In *the chat*

Each step names the tabs, such as `Moved 2 tabs to a new task, Hotels`. Click a tab in a step to go to it, or a task to see it in the sidebar. The arrow at the end of a step shows the tabs it moved or closed.

A [scheduled chat](./scheduled-chats.mdx) can tidy your tabs too, such as every Friday. It never switches your window to a tab, and a close waits for you in Auto.
